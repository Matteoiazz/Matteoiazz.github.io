Scope: home page (app/page.tsx) and case study pages; visitor mode Experience. Audience: Italian recruiters, IT+EN. Job: reach a real project in seconds, then contact or CV.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The first site's dark liquid-glass world, carried to its peak and filled with the portfolio's interactive details: the chrome flower stands in front of a giant name like the subject of a magazine cover. Refuses the white editorial grid with black chapters that read as the reference site's language.
OWN-WORLD: Near-black ground (#0b0b0c), the chrome flower render (cut out with its own alpha) as the only object, chrome lettering only on the chapter words, liquid glass (backdrop blur, top highlight, cursor light) on floating chrome: capsule bar, menu, dialog, lens cursor; flat glass on cards and boards. Archivo variable for names and headings, Geist for text, Geist Mono for data. Rounded glass (18-26px), pill buttons, white primary pill.
STORY: Visitor meets the name behind the flower, learns "junior full-stack, heading to AI", runs through three featured projects sideways, scans the departure board of all seven, meets the person (chrome CHI SONO, manifesto, skills matrix), the path, and writes or downloads the CV; the page closes on the chrome signature.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed dark; MATTEO / IAZZOLINO across the width in off-white Archivo, letters emerging from blur; the cut-out chrome flower in front at center-right covering part of the letters, tilting to the pointer; split-flap role line top-left, intro top-right, white "Vedi i progetti" pill bottom-left. On phones the flower sits above the name, its lower petals over the top of MATTEO.
FORM: user-pinned direction (return to the first site's liquid glass, chosen in the style form: Vetro scuro, Nome dietro il fiore, Vetro che si appanna, giant words kept, all details kept); supersedes the rolled direction of seed 18171cd7. Signature interaction: the fog bands at the screen edges where content blurs as it leaves, plus the pointer-reactive variable name.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

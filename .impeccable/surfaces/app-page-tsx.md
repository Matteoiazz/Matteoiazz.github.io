## Direction contract
THESIS: The portfolio as a Crouwel-style type specimen: a visible construction grid on which the name and every section are built cell by cell. Refuses the dark dev portfolio with cards and the terminal costume.
OWN-WORLD: Pure white paper (#ffffff) and ink (#0b0b0b), hairline grid rules at 12 columns, chapters inverting to black in grid-cell blocks. Archivo variable (width and weight axes) for all type, Geist Mono only for data (clock, years, counts). No radius beyond 2px, no shadows except the preview card, no colour.
STORY: Visitor meets the name, learns "junior full-stack, heading to AI", sees three featured projects in a pinned horizontal run, scans the departure-board index of all seven, meets the person (manifesto, skills matrix, path) and writes or downloads the CV.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed grid. MATTEO / IAZZOLINO across the full width at ~19vw, assembled cell by cell on load; letters swell in width and weight under the pointer. Below, a split-flap line rotating the role, then a row: Cosenza live clock, availability, IT/EN, "Vedi i progetti" primary action bottom-left.
FORM: challenger crouwel-grid-specimen (won both axes; user chose it on my recommendation), seed key 18171cd7. Signature interaction: pointer-reactive variable-font name plus split-flap (Solari) rotating lines; motion grammar: cell-by-cell builds and grid-block inversions.

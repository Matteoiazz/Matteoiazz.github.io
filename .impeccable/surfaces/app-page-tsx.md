## Direction contract
THESIS: The reference site's cleanliness (white page, one grotesk, ink with one grey voice, airy offset columns, plain project media, editorial project pages) made Matteo's own: his name opens the statement, his work is already in the first viewport, the stack is proven project by project, and the page closes in black with his name across the width. Refuses chrome, glass, split-flap tiles, the specimen grid, numbered section labels, kickers and freelance pricing.
OWN-WORLD: Paper white #fff, ink #0a0a0a, one grey #737373 as the second voice of every heading, hairlines #e8e8e8, a single black chapter for the close. Mona Sans variable throughout (GitHub's face, where his code lives): 500 for headings at -0.03em, 400 for text; the width axis only on the closing name and on the index title under the pointer. The cut-out chrome flower as the one object, resting on white. Media on a #f4f4f4 stage with 14px corners, black pill for the primary action, hairline pills for the rest, Lucide arrows.
STORY: The visitor reads "Matteo Iazzolino, Junior Full-Stack Developer, from database to interface, heading into AI", sees three real apps right under it, scans all seven projects with a cover under the pointer, opens one and watches the cover become the page, then meets the person, the stack matrix and the path, and writes, copies the email or downloads the CV in the black close.
FIRST VIEWPORT: White. Slim bar: name left; Progetti, Chi sono, Percorso, Contatti and IT/EN right. Two-tone statement over the left seven columns at about 4.75rem (name and role in ink, the rest in grey), a two-line intro and two pills (Vedi i progetti, Curriculum) under it; the flower floats in the right columns. The three featured project panels start near the bottom and peek above the fold. On phones the statement leads, the flower sits smaller under the pills, the panels stack.
